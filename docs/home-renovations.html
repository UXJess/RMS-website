Important Notes

Honest About
What We Do & Don't Do.

Certified Trades

Work that legally requires a licensed plumber, electrician, or carpenter will be outsourced to certified tradespeople. This protects your home's insurance coverage and all applicable warranties.

Permits & Inspections

Permits, design, and required inspections are primarily the homeowner's responsibility. If permit assistance is needed, this can be discussed during the initial consultation.

Safety First

RMS is operated by a certified Construction Safety Officer (CSO) and OFA Level 3 first aid professional. Safe work practices are built into every project — your home is treated with care.
