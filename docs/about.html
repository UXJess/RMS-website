How We Started

Raised in the
Industry.
Grown Into Three Trades.

Rocky Mountain Solutions grew from years of hands-on construction work and a clear observation: the demand for qualified safety professionals was growing, but the gap between what sites needed and what they had was real. After earning a Construction Safety Officer certification and OFA Level 3 first aid designation, filling that gap became the foundation of the business.

What started as field safety coverage expanded naturally. The same contractors and property owners who relied on safety support began asking about maintenance, repairs, and renovations. Strata councils needed a dependable trade they could call. Homeowners wanted someone who would show up, communicate clearly, and do the work properly. Rocky Mountain Solutions now operates across all three — construction safety compliance, strata maintenance, and home renovations — under one roof, with one point of contact.

The standard doesn't change by service. Show up when you say you will, do the work properly, communicate clearly, and leave the client better off than you found them. That's the only principle this company runs on.

Credentials & Certifications

Every Credential
Earned on the Ground.

Primary Designation

Construction Safety Officer

CSO designation providing full authority to conduct inspections, direct workers on safety procedures, and liaise with WorkSafeBC officers on your behalf.

First Aid Certification

OFA Level 3 — Advanced First Aid

Occupational First Aid Level 3. The highest field-level certification in BC — qualified to manage medical emergencies, stabilize workers, and coordinate with EMS before transport.

Regulatory Compliance

WorkSafeBC Compliant Services

All services delivered in full alignment with WorkSafeBC regulations. Inspections, documentation, and corrective action plans meet BC Occupational Health & Safety standards.

Audit Support

COR® Certificate of Recognition

Support for companies pursuing or maintaining COR® status. Audit preparation, safety management system documentation, and ongoing compliance records kept current.

Ongoing Training

Certifications are maintained and expanded through accredited training providers. Field experience continues to build alongside formal credentials — because a safety officer who stops learning is one who stops seeing hazards.
